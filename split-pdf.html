How to split a PDF

1. Upload the PDF document you want to split.

2. Once the page count is loaded, enter the pages or page ranges you want to extract (e.g., 1-3, 5).

3. Click "Split PDF" and the new document will be generated locally in your browser.

4. Download the extracted pages.
