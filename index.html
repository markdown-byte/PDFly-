Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our technology, privacy, and free service.

Are my files or sensitive documents ever uploaded to your servers?

No, never. All document manipulation runs entirely within your browser's local sandbox via client-side JavaScript and WebAssembly. No files, text, images, or metadata are ever transmitted across the internet to our servers or any third parties.

Is PDF Toolkit truly free to use?

Yes, 100%. All tools are completely free without required sign-ups, credit cards, or hidden limits. The platform is sustainably supported by non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdSense.

Will you add watermarks or banners to my downloaded PDFs?

No. We respect your document integrity. We never stamp watermarks, brand logos, or footer notices onto your files.

Are there file size limits when processing documents?

Because processing takes place in your local computer or phone RAM, limits depend primarily on your device hardware rather than server restrictions. Typical files up to 100MB process smoothly without delays.
