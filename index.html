PDF Guides & Tips

How to merge PDF files?

Simply select our Merge tool, drag and drop your PDFs into the browser, arrange them in your desired order, and click merge. All processing happens on your device.

Are my files secure?

Yes. Our tools operate entirely in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server, ensuring 100% privacy and fast processing times.

Do I need an account to use these tools?

No, PDF Toolkit is entirely free to use with no registration required and no watermarks added to your documents.
